# Forms Design

## Equipment Registration Form

Purpose:
Register new laboratory equipment.

Fields:

- Official Inventory Number
- Internal Lab Number
- Equipment Name
- Brand
- Model
- Location
- Operational Status
- Category

Actions:

- Save Equipment
- Cancel

Validation:

- Equipment Name required
- Location required
- Operational Status required
- Category required
- At least one inventory identifier required

---
